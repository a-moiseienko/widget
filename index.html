A page with the chat widget on it

This is a stand-in for your website. The chat button sits in the bottom-right corner and talks to the backend at http://localhost:8080.

The backend answers these questions:

  • Hello
  • What are your opening hours?
  • How can I contact support?
  • How do I reset my password?
  • Do you offer refunds?
  • Where is my order?
  • Thanks

Anything else gets a fallback answer. Messages are limited to 250 characters.
